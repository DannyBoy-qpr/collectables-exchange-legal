Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Collectables Exchange ("CX", "we", "us") collects when you use our mobile application and website (the "Platform"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your data. We are committed to handling your data carefully and transparently, in line with the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Collectables Exchange is the data controller responsible for your personal data.

Contact: privacy@collectablesexchange.com

2. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: email address, username, password (hashed), profile photo, bio, location string, date you joined.
  • Verification data: for tiered KYC — government ID, address, date of birth (only collected when you request a higher trust tier).
  • Listing data: photos and descriptions of collectables you list, cert numbers, prices.
  • Trade data: escrow records, shipping addresses, tracking numbers, confirmations.
  • Payment data: we use Stripe to process payments. We never see or store your full card details. We do store a Stripe customer ID to manage subscriptions.
  • Communication data: direct messages between you and other users, support emails.
  • Usage data: device type, OS version, in-app actions (which screens you visit), crash reports.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Operate the Platform (sign you in, show listings, route messages, run escrow).
  • Verify identity for tiered trust tiers and AML compliance.
  • Process payments and subscriptions via Stripe.
  • Send transactional notifications (offers, fee payments, shipment status, dispute updates).
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and counterfeit listings.
  • Improve the Platform — anonymised, aggregated usage data only.
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g. responding to law enforcement requests).

4. Legal bases

We rely on the following lawful bases for processing your data:

  • Contract: processing necessary to provide the Platform you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: fraud prevention, security, and improving our service.
  • Consent: for optional things like push notifications and marketing emails (you can withdraw at any time).
  • Legal obligation: KYC, AML, and tax compliance.

5. Who we share data with

We share data with these categories of third parties:

  • Supabase — our backend and database host (data stored in EU region).
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Google (Gemini) — our trade fairness AI runs on Google's Gemini API. We send anonymised listing details only; no user identities are sent.
  • Sentry — anonymised crash reporting (no PII).
  • Shipping carriers — Royal Mail, ParcelForce, or DPD for sending verified slabs.
  • Grader databases — PSA, BGS, CGC for verifying cert numbers (we share the cert number only).
  • Law enforcement — only where legally required.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

6. International transfers

Some of our processors (Stripe, Google) are located outside the UK and EU. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions issued by the UK government.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data while your account is open and for up to 6 years after closure to comply with tax and financial record-keeping requirements. Trade and escrow records are kept for 6 years. Direct messages are kept for 2 years after the conversation's last message, then deleted.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for processing based on consent).
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights email privacy@collectablesexchange.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS in transit, encryption at rest, hashed passwords (bcrypt), and row-level security on our database. Payment data never touches our servers — it is handled directly by Stripe. Despite this, no system is 100% secure; please use a strong password and enable any future MFA option we add.

10. Cookies and tracking

The mobile app does not use cookies. We use device identifiers for crash reporting and to deliver push notifications (if you opt in). The CX website uses minimal first-party cookies for authentication only; no third-party advertising trackers are deployed.

11. Children

The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account please contact us and we will remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-app at least 14 days before they take effect.

13. Contact
